================================================================
          APEX WIRELESS ANC HEADPHONES (X100-PRO)
                   USER MANUAL & QUICK START
================================================================

1. PAIRING INSTRUCTIONS
   - Hold power button for 3 seconds until LED flashes Blue/Red.
   - On your device, select "Apex X100-Pro" from Bluetooth menu.
   - Multipoint Pairing: Hold Bluetooth button 2 sec to add second device.

2. ACTIVE NOISE CANCELLATION (ANC)
   - Press ANC button on left ear cup to cycle modes:
     * ANC High (Noise blocking up to 35dB)
     * Transparency Mode (Amplify ambient audio)
     * ANC Off

3. BATTERY & CHARGING
   - 40 Hours playtime with ANC enabled (55 Hours ANC Off).
   - Fast Charging: 10 mins USB-C charge yields 4 hours playtime.

4. SAFETY & COMPLIANCE
   - Water Resistance: IPX4 splashproof rating.
   - Operating Temp: -10°C to 45°C.
   - Do not expose battery to extreme heat or direct sunlight.
